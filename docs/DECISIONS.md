- 2026-11-24  step3e comparisons and metrics, fixed before any step3e result. Scored with the existing scorer, gate (rodrigues2021, 6 dB) and windows; learned rows mean ± SD over S1–S3.
  - (a) Schedule effect: L1supE2E − L1sup and L2supE2E − L2sup (same seeds, folds and initial weights).
  - (b) Pretraining effect against the end-to-end baseline: L1 − L1supE2E and L2 − L2supE2E, reported next to the existing L1 − L1sup and L2 − L2sup. The pretraining effect is reported against both supervised arms; neither is chosen after seeing results.

Training: best epoch and best validation loss per run.

Known confound in (a): 90 instead of 70 full-network epochs. Known differences that remain in (b): layer decay 0.65 and no weight decay on biases in the pretrained arm, its 20 head-only epochs, and its single unseeded pre-training run per backbone.
